Add up your numbers without opening a spreadsheet or a calculator

Tally prints the total and the average of any list of numbers, on one line, safe to pipe into anything else you run.

How it works

Give it numbers and it answers with the total and the average. Nothing is stored and nothing is sent anywhere. It runs the same on a laptop, a server and in a build log.
